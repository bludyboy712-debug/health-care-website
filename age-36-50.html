Ages 36–50

Health guide for Adults

Metabolism slows down, so heart, blood sugar and bone health need more attention.

Diet and nutrition

Keep your weight steady and protect your heart with fibre, lean protein, less salt and less sugar.

Eating guide

  • Choose whole grains (atta roti, oats, daliya) over white bread and white rice.
  • Eat fish, chicken or lentils, and use olive or canola oil in small amounts.
  • Keep salt below 5 g (about 1 teaspoon) a day and cut down on sweets and sugary tea.
Water: About 8–9 glasses a day, more in summer.
Limit: Refined flour, fried snacks, sweet drinks and extra salt.

Sample day

Daily health routine

Daily habits

  1. 1Sleep 7–9 hours and keep a fixed bedtime.
  2. 2Walk or cycle 30 minutes on most days and do strength exercises twice a week.
  3. 3Stretch daily and take breaks from sitting to protect your back.
  4. 4Make time for rest and family, and talk about stress early.
Check-ups: Check blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and eyes every year, and ask your doctor which screenings you need.

Skin care routine

Skin care basics

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturiser
  • Sunscreen

Morning

  1. Cleanse gently.
  2. Apply an antioxidant (vitamin C) serum and a moisturiser.
  3. Use SPF 50 sunscreen every day.

Night

  1. Cleanse and apply a toner if you like.
  2. Use a retinol or niacinamide serum. Start slowly, a few nights a week.
  3. Apply a richer night cream and an eye cream.

Weekly care

  • Exfoliate gently once a week and use a hydrating mask.

Avoid

  • Smoking, which speeds up wrinkles.
  • Hot water and harsh scrubs that dry the skin.
Patch-test new products on a small area first. See a dermatologist if a problem lasts more than a few weeks.
