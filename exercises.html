Wall Push-ups

2 × 10–12 repsBeginner

Stand an arm's length from a wall, bend your elbows to bring your chest close, then push back. A gentle start for chest and arms.

Bodyweight Squats

2 × 10–12 repsBeginner

Feet shoulder-width apart, sit back as if onto a chair, keep your chest up and push through your heels.

Glute Bridge

2 × 10–12 repsBeginner

Lie on your back with knees bent, lift your hips and squeeze your glutes at the top.

Marching in Place

2 × 30 secondsBeginner

Lift your knees one at a time at a steady pace while swinging your arms. A simple cardio warm-up.

Knee Plank

2 × 15–20 secondsBeginner

Hold a straight line from head to knees on your forearms and keep your core tight.

Push-ups

3 × 8–12 repsIntermediate

Hands under shoulders and body straight, lower your chest to the floor and push back up. Do them on your knees if needed.

Alternating Lunges

3 × 10 reps per sideIntermediate

Step forward, lower until both knees bend to about 90 degrees, then push back to standing.

Plank

3 × 30–45 secondsIntermediate

Hold a straight line from head to heels on your forearms or hands. Do not let your hips sag.

Mountain Climbers

3 × 30 secondsIntermediate

From a high plank, drive your knees towards your chest one after the other at a quick pace.

Chair Dips

3 × 10–12 repsIntermediate

Hands on a sturdy chair behind you, bend your elbows to lower your body and press up. Works the back of the arms.

Burpees

3 × 10–15 repsAdvanced

Squat, jump your feet back to a plank, return and jump up. A full-body workout that raises your heart rate.

Jump Squats

3 × 12–15 repsAdvanced

Squat down, then jump up and land softly with bent knees. Builds leg power.

Pike Push-ups

3 × 8–12 repsAdvanced

With hips high in an inverted V, bend your elbows to lower your head towards the floor. Targets the shoulders.

Hollow Hold

3 × 30 secondsAdvanced

Lie on your back, lift your shoulders and straight legs off the floor and keep your lower back pressed down.

Split Squats

3 × 8–10 reps per sideAdvanced

Place one foot behind you on a chair or bed and lower your front knee. Builds strong legs and balance.

15-minute starter workout

Do each move for its set, then rest for 30 seconds before the next one.

  1. Marching in Place
  2. Bodyweight Squats
  3. Glute Bridge
  4. Knee Plank
  5. Wall Push-ups

Before you begin

  • Warm up with 5 minutes of marching or easy stretching.
  • Choose good form over speed and keep your back straight.
  • Drink water and rest 30 to 60 seconds between sets.
  • Exercise 3 to 5 days a week and give your body rest days.
Stay safe: Warm up for 5 minutes and stretch afterwards. Rest 30–60 seconds between sets and keep your back straight. Stop if you feel sharp pain, and ask a doctor first if you have a heart, joint or blood pressure condition.
