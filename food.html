Healthy food guide

Simple foods you already know, grouped by what they do for your body.

Fruits and vegetables

Give vitamins, minerals and fibre. Aim for 5 portions a day in different colours.

Protein foods

Build and repair muscle and keep you full for longer.

Whole grains

Release energy slowly and give fibre for digestion.

Dairy and calcium

Support strong bones and teeth. Choose plain, low-sugar options.

Healthy fats

Protect the heart and brain when eaten in small amounts.

Drinks

Water should be your main drink. Sip through the day.

Easy swaps

Soda or cold drinkWater with lemon and mint
Fried samosa or pakoraRoasted chana or baked snacks
White breadWhole wheat roti or brown bread
ChipsRoasted nuts or makhana
Packaged juiceA whole fruit
Sugary teaTea with less or no sugar

Eat smart

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables.
  • Do not skip breakfast.
  • Eat slowly and stop when you are about 80% full.
  • Cook with less oil. Grill, boil or bake instead of frying.
  • Read food labels and check the sugar and salt.

Eat less of

  • Sugary drinks
  • Deep-fried snacks
  • Processed meats
  • Extra salt
  • Sweets and bakery items
