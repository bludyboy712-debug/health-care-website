About HealthyWorld

HealthyWorld is a free guide to everyday health for every age, written in plain language and available in four languages.

Our mission

Good health advice should be easy to find, easy to understand and available in the language you speak at home. We bring diet, daily routines, skin care, yoga and home exercise together in one calm, simple place.

What you will find here

Six age-wise guides with meal ideas, daily habits and skin care steps. Fifteen yoga poses and fifteen home exercises with animated demos from beginner to advanced. A food guide with everyday foods, easy swaps and eating tips.

How we write our guides

We write in plain language and check our advice against widely accepted public health guidance, such as World Health Organization recommendations on physical activity, sleep and salt. We prefer small, realistic steps over extreme plans.

Who this is for

Students, parents, working adults and older family members who want practical health information without jargon. Everyone can use it free of charge.

Important limits

HealthyWorld shares general information. It is not a medical service and does not replace a doctor, dietitian or physiotherapist. Please read our medical disclaimer.
