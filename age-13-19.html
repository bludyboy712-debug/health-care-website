Diet and nutrition

Fuel growth, studies and sport with regular meals instead of skipping meals or crash dieting.

Eating guide

  • Never skip breakfast. It helps you focus in class.
  • Get iron from daal, beans, meat and spinach, and calcium from milk and yogurt.
  • Choose home snacks like nuts, fruit or chana chaat over fast food and energy drinks.
Water: About 7–8 glasses a day, and extra during sport.
Limit: Energy drinks, soda, frequent fast food and crash diets.

Sample day

Daily health routine

Daily habits

  1. 1Sleep 8–10 hours and keep your phone away from your bed.
  2. 2Be active for at least 60 minutes a day with a sport or workout you enjoy.
  3. 3Talk to a parent, teacher or doctor if stress or low mood lasts more than two weeks.
  4. 4Shower daily, wear clean clothes and stay away from tobacco and vapes.
Check-ups: Yearly check-up, an eye test, a dental visit every 6 months, and an iron check if you often feel tired.

Skin care routine

Skin care basics

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturiser
  • Sunscreen

Morning

  1. Wash with a gentle gel or foaming cleanser.
  2. Apply an oil-free (non-comedogenic) moisturiser.
  3. Use SPF 30+ sunscreen, even on cloudy days.

Night

  1. Wash your face again, especially after sport or sweating.
  2. Dab benzoyl peroxide 2.5% or salicylic acid on spots if needed.
  3. Moisturise lightly with an oil-free product.

Weekly care

  • Change your pillowcase and towel once a week.
  • Exfoliate gently once a week at most, and only if your skin is not irritated.

Avoid

  • Picking or squeezing pimples. It causes scars.
  • Harsh scrubs, toothpaste on spots and heavy makeup.
  • Washing more than twice a day.
Patch-test new products on a small area first. See a dermatologist if a problem lasts more than a few weeks.
