Diet and nutrition

Keep muscles strong and energy steady with small, protein-rich, easy-to-digest meals.

Eating guide

  • Eat protein at each meal (eggs, daal, yogurt, fish, chicken) to protect muscle.
  • Have 4–5 smaller meals if big meals feel heavy, and choose soft, easy-to-chew foods.
  • Include calcium, vitamin D and B12 foods and ask your doctor about supplements.
Water: About 6–8 glasses a day. Sip regularly, because thirst fades with age.
Limit: Extra salt, fried food, sugary drinks and skipped meals.

Sample day

Daily health routine

Daily habits

  1. 1Walk 20–30 minutes a day, split into short walks if needed.
  2. 2Do gentle balance and leg-strength exercises to prevent falls.
  3. 3Take medicines on time and review them with your doctor regularly.
  4. 4Keep floors clear, use night lights and stay in touch with family and friends.
Check-ups: See your doctor at least twice a year. Check blood pressure, sugar, eyes, hearing and teeth, and take the yearly flu vaccine and others your doctor advises.

Skin care routine

Skin care basics

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturiser
  • Sunscreen

Morning

  1. Wash with lukewarm water and a fragrance-free cream cleanser.
  2. Pat dry and apply a thick moisturiser right away.
  3. Use SPF 30+ on exposed skin and a lip balm.

Night

  1. Cleanse gently and pat dry, never rub.
  2. Apply a rich ceramide or urea cream to arms, legs and hands.
  3. Use a little petroleum jelly on dry heels and elbows.

Weekly care

  • Check your skin and feet, and show any new, changing or non-healing spot to a doctor.

Avoid

  • Hot water, harsh soaps and strong scrubs.
  • Scratching dry, itchy skin. See a doctor if itching continues.
Patch-test new products on a small area first. See a dermatologist if a problem lasts more than a few weeks.
