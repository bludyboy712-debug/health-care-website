Health guide for Mature adults

Bones, muscles and skin need extra care, and prevention matters most.

Diet and nutrition

Protect your heart, bones and muscles with calcium, vitamin D, fibre and enough protein.

Eating guide

  • Eat calcium and vitamin D foods daily: milk, yogurt, small fish, eggs and a little morning sunshine.
  • Add fibre from vegetables, fruit, daal and whole grains for digestion and heart health.
  • Reduce salt and sugar, and take healthy fats from olive oil, nuts and fish.
Water: About 8 glasses a day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
Limit: Salty pickles and snacks, sweets, fried food and heavy meals at night.

Sample day

Daily health routine

Daily habits

  1. 1Sleep 7–9 hours and keep a calm bedtime routine.
  2. 2Walk 30 minutes daily and add light strength and balance exercises 2–3 times a week.
  3. 3Keep blood pressure and sugar under control and take medicines as advised.
  4. 4Stay social and keep your mind active with reading, prayer, hobbies or gardening.
Check-ups: Yearly checks for blood pressure, sugar, cholesterol, eyes and hearing. Ask about bone density, screenings and vaccines.

Skin care routine

Skin care basics

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturiser
  • Sunscreen

Morning

  1. Use a creamy, gentle cleanser.
  2. Apply a hydrating serum (hyaluronic acid) and a ceramide moisturiser.
  3. Use SPF 50 sunscreen on face, neck and hands.

Night

  1. Cleanse gently with lukewarm water.
  2. Use a low-strength retinoid or peptide serum a few nights a week.
  3. Finish with a rich night cream.

Weekly care

  • Exfoliate gently once a week or less, because skin is thinner now.
  • Look over your skin each month and show any new or changing mole to a doctor.

Avoid

  • Long hot showers and harsh soaps.
  • Smoking and too much sun.
Patch-test new products on a small area first. See a dermatologist if a problem lasts more than a few weeks.
