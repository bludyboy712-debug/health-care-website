Medical disclaimer

Last updated: 11 October 2026

General information only

Everything on HealthyWorld is for general education and information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for the care of a qualified health professional.

Talk to a professional

Always speak to a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional before you change your diet, start an exercise programme or begin a new skin care routine. This is especially important if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, take medicines or have an injury.

Exercise and yoga

Physical activity carries some risk. Warm up, move within your comfort, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or breathlessness. Advanced poses such as the headstand should be learned with a qualified teacher.

Food and nutrition

Nutrition needs differ from person to person. Allergies, intolerances and medical conditions can change what is safe for you to eat. Our meal ideas are examples, not a prescription.

Accuracy

We work to keep our information accurate and up to date, but health knowledge changes and we cannot guarantee that everything is complete or error free.

Emergencies

If you think you or someone else has a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not rely on this website.
