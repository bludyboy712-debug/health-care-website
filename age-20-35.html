Diet and nutrition

Keep your energy steady and your weight healthy with balanced plates and home cooking.

Eating guide

  • Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruit, a quarter with protein and a quarter with whole grains.
  • Add protein (egg, daal, chicken, fish, yogurt) to every meal.
  • Cook at home more often and use less oil.
Water: About 8–10 glasses a day (2–2.5 litres).
Limit: Sugary drinks, deep-fried snacks and heavy late-night meals.

Sample day

Daily health routine

Daily habits

  1. 1Sleep 7–9 hours at a regular time.
  2. 2Exercise 150 minutes a week and take a short walk every hour of desk work.
  3. 3Manage stress with deep breathing, prayer or meditation, hobbies and time with friends.
  4. 4Avoid smoking and keep screens away for the last hour before bed.
Check-ups: Check blood pressure, blood sugar and weight once a year, and visit the dentist every 6 months.

Skin care routine

Skin care basics

  • Cleanser
  • Serum
  • Moisturiser
  • Sunscreen

Morning

  1. Cleanse with a gentle cleanser.
  2. Apply a vitamin C serum (optional) and a light moisturiser.
  3. Finish with SPF 30–50 sunscreen and reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors.

Night

  1. Remove sunscreen and makeup first, then cleanse again.
  2. Start retinol 2–3 nights a week at a low strength (skip it in pregnancy).
  3. Apply moisturiser.

Weekly care

  • Exfoliate gently once or twice a week with a mild AHA or BHA, not a harsh scrub.

Avoid

  • Sleeping with makeup on.
  • Sunbathing and skipping sunscreen.
Patch-test new products on a small area first. See a dermatologist if a problem lasts more than a few weeks.
