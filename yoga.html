8–10 roundsBeginner

On hands and knees, arch your back as you inhale and round it as you exhale. Warms up the spine.

Balasana
30–60 secondsBeginner

Sit back on your heels, fold forward and rest your forehead down. Relaxes the back and calms the mind.

3 × 15–20 secondsBeginner

Before you begin

  • Practise on a mat or a non-slip surface, ideally on an empty stomach.
  • Breathe slowly through your nose and let the breath lead the movement.
  • Go to a comfortable stretch, never to pain, and stay within your own range.
  • Finish with a few quiet breaths in a seated pose.
Stay safe: Warm up first and never force a pose. Breathe slowly and skip any pose that hurts. Ask a doctor first if you are pregnant or have back, neck, heart or blood pressure problems. Learn advanced poses such as the headstand with a qualified teacher.
