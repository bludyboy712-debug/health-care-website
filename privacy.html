Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Overview

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information HealthyWorld uses and how. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for personal health details.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name or contact details unless you email us. The age and language you choose are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so the site can remember them. They are not sent to us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, for security and reliability.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads served by third-party vendors such as Google. These vendors use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. These tools may use cookies and collect anonymous usage data.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, so please read their policies.

Children

HealthyWorld is meant to be read by families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should guide children when they use the internet.

Your choices

You can clear the data saved in your browser at any time, block cookies, or ask us to answer questions about this policy by email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
